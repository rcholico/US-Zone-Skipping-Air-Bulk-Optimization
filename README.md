# ✈️ US Zone Skipping & Air-Bulk Linehaul Optimization (Origen: San Diego, California, USA)

**Parcel Network Redesign & End-to-End Transit Lead Time Reduction for Eastern US Markets**

An executive parcel analytics and network injection framework created for a Medical Tech leader to analyze 149,000+ monthly shipments originating from the San Diego / Tijuana (TJD) manufacturing hub, optimizing Zone 6–8 deliveries into the Eastern, Central, and Northeast US customer regions.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Strategic Problem Statement

Shipments bound for East Coast and Northeast customers were experiencing total transit times of **9 to 13 days** due to multi-stop handling (TJD $\rightarrow$ DFW Hub $\rightarrow$ East Coast Last Mile). This project evaluated linehaul zone-skipping strategies - combining FTL Intermodal and Bulk Airfreight to bypass intermediate sortation hubs and drop packages directly into regional parcel carrier hubs (Indianapolis, Pittsburgh, Baltimore).

---

## 📊 Dataset & Parcel Volume Analysis

* **Sample Dataset:** 149,374 package shipments across 8 parcel carrier service levels (Ground, Home Delivery, Priority/Standard Overnight, 2Day, Express Saver).
* **Top Destination States Analyzed:** Texas (11.4%), California (7.8%), Wisconsin (5.9%), Florida (5.8%), Ohio (4.8%), North Carolina (4.3%), Massachusetts (3.7%), New York (3.5%).
* **Regional Distribution Focus:** Northeast (High-density zone 7–8 delivery zone) and Central US.

---

## 🚚 Network Architecture: Current vs. Proposed Zone-Skip Model

| Metric / Stage | Baseline Flow (Multi-Hub) | Proposed Zone-Skip Flow (Air/Intermodal Injection) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Origin / Linehaul** | Tijuana (TJD) $\rightarrow$ DFW Hub via Truck (5–7 Days) | TJD $\rightarrow$ Direct Air / Intermodal to Regional Hub (1–2 Days) |
| **Intermediate Sorting**| DFW Warehouse Processing (1–2 Days) | **Bypassed / Zone Skipped** |
| **Last-Mile Delivery** | Parcel Ground / Home Delivery (3–4 Days) | Parcel Carrier Local Regional Hub Injection (2–3 Days) |
| **Total Customer Transit**| **9 – 13 Days**[cite: 6] | **4 – 6 Days (60% Lead-Time Reduction)** |

---

## 🔑 Key Business Impact & Results

* **End-to-End Lead Time Reduction:** Cut total order-to-delivery time for Northeast customers from **9–13 days down to 4–6 days**, matching the performance of a dedicated localized warehouse without the real estate overhead.
* **Trident Hub Project Evaluation:** Mapped 1-to-5 day FedEx Home Delivery delivery contours across **San Diego (CA)**, **Fort Worth (TX)**, **Indianapolis (IN)**, **Pittsburgh (PA)**, and **Baltimore (MD)**.
* **Operational Working Capital:** Significantly reduced working capital locked in transit and lowered DFW facility headcount requirements (FTE reduction).
